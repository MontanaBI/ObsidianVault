https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6Mfl1OywM8 
![[PARA overview image.png]]

**Projects** 
Most actionable  
Has a Deadline  
Completed over multiple sessions  
E.g. writing a blog post, planning a trip  

**Areas**
Role or responsibility  
Has a standard to maintain  
Never complete, important all the time  
E.g. exercise, gift ideas, holiday ideas  

**Resources**
Any topic of on going interest  
Catch all  
That isn't an project or area, may be activated in future  
Eg random interest / hobbies  
Books you have read 

**Archives**
Inactive items from Projects, Areas and Resources
